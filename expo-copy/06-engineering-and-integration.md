**Controls platform**
Industry-standard PLC platforms. Customer-specified electrical components accommodated
on request. Controls packages designed to run independent of existing line
controls; specific network and safety integration available on request.

**Measurement**
3D measuring technology models an existing machine setup precisely, so new parts
fit current core plates without permanent alterations.

**Testing before shipment**
All systems tested with video before shipment · many machines additionally run
factory-specific testing prior to FAT · plant environment replicated with the
customer's own containers · requested failure modes simulated, plus failures the
engineering team recommends testing · results tracked and reviewed across machines

Installation, training, and both remote and on-site service in the event of
disruptions to production.

## 9. Booth answers

**"What does the line evaluation cost?"**
Nothing. It's complimentary. Our team walks the operation and reports where the container is costing you throughput.

**"We already have equipment from four different suppliers."**
That's typical, and it's the work. We design the layout around what you run and integrate the other manufacturers' machines so the line behaves as one system.

**"Who does your controls?"**
Our own controls engineers, in house, on industry-standard PLC platforms. Customer-specified components can be accommodated.

**"Will it interfere with our existing line controls?"**
Controls packages are designed to be independent of them. Where you want integration into your network and safety systems instead, that's specified up front.

**"Can we see it run before it ships?"**
Yes. Every system is tested with video before shipment, and we can replicate your plant environment for factory testing — including simulating the failure modes you want to see.

**"Can we get line data out of it?"**
Ethernet communication into plant-wide networks is standard on the platforms we design with, so efficiency and productivity metrics can be tracked rather than estimated.
