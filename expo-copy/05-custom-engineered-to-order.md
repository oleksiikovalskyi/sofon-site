## 8. Industry proof points

- **Alcohol.** A dual reject configuration built for a high-speed 50 ml spirits line where fallen bottles stacked on each other — one unit clearing the top bottle, a second clearing what remained.
- **Beverage.** A custom system creating five-pack can stacks across multiple lanes; another turning and dividing warm-filled plastic juice bottles.
- **Dairy.** Systems built to divide, stack and group single-serve cups for sleeving and multi-packing.
- **Food.** One system that divides, stacks and groups cups at 9,600 containers per hour — three handling steps solved by a single machine.
- **Household products.** A custom packaging system for trigger spray bottles running at 19,200 containers per hour.
- **Personal care.** Grouping systems presenting glass bottles for sleeving at 7,200 containers per hour, where the container is too delicate for standard handling.
- **Automotive and petroleum.** An integrated cylinder handling, inspection and mating solution running propane cylinders at 3,600 containers per hour; an indexing system orienting and transferring 890 ml sealant tubes.

## 9. Booth answers

**"Our container is odd."**
That's the starting condition for most of what our custom division builds. Bring the container to the stand or send it to us — the design starts from the physical object.

**"We only have this much floor space."**
Constraints are part of the specification, not an obstacle to it. Floor space, existing conveyor heights and the machines either side all go into the design.

**"How custom is custom?"**
Unit of one. Machines that have never existed before, built to solve one challenge on one line.

**"We can't afford a custom machine."**
Then start with the challenge rather than the machine. Customization scales — the design begins from the most cost-effective route to the result you need.

**"We'd have to shut the line down."**
Designs are built to create the least possible impact on existing production. That constraint goes in at the start, not at installation.

**"Who handles the other equipment on the line?"**
We do, if you want. Integrating other OEMs' machines and designing the conveyor layout around them is part of the same scope.
