## 8. Industry proof points

- **Beverage.** When newly supplied change parts failed to fit, the response ran from purchase order to shipment in seven business days — on-site measurement, design validation, production and international shipping coordinated in parallel.
- **Alcohol.** Spirits and wine lines run reject and handling equipment integrated into existing controls, where service means understanding both our equipment and the architecture around it.
- **Dairy.** Washdown environments accelerate wear on contact parts; a stocked spare parts list is the difference between a shift interruption and a scheduled swap.
- **Food.** High-SKU plants change over constantly, and training operators on changeover best practice reduces the wear that generates service calls.
- **Household products.** Multi-size lines benefit most from the assess-maintain-improve cycle, because the equipment is being adjusted more often than single-SKU lines.
- **Personal care.** Container finish is the product; regular inspection of contact parts protects appearance before a customer complaint does it for you.
- **Automotive and petroleum.** Heavy containers wear guides and screws faster, which is exactly the case a scheduled maintenance plan is built for.

## 9. Booth answers

**"We didn't buy this machine from you."**
Parts and service still apply. We work on equipment across the market, and our applications team can evaluate what you're running.

**"Our maintenance team is two people."**
That's the most common reason customers take a maintenance agreement. The visits do the work and train your two people while they're on site.

**"How many visits do we get?"**
Between four and eight over a two-year agreement, depending on the plan. Bi-annual, quarterly and custom structures are available.

**"What happens after a visit?"**
You get a recommended spare parts list of the wear items we saw — so the next stoppage is a part off your own shelf rather than a phone call.

**"Can you help remotely?"**
Yes. Remote diagnosis and remote service are part of the offering, and remote start-up support exists for new equipment.

**"We've lost the manual."**
Get in touch and we'll send another copy. Manuals are written specifically for each machine, covering changeover, preventative maintenance and mounting.

**"Can you train our operators?"**
On site or remotely, for both operator and maintenance teams — best practices, troubleshooting and the areas that matter most for uptime.
