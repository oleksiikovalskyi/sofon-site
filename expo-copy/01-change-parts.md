## 8. Industry proof points

- **Alcohol.** Wine and spirits lines run some of the most part-dependent equipment there is — corkers, capsulators, foilers and monoblocks. We build parts for all of them.
- **Beverage.** When a supplier's change parts arrived with fitment problems, we went from purchase order to shipment in seven business days, including on-site measurement and design validation.
- **Dairy.** Cups, tubs and gable-top cartons change size constantly. Retrofit parts let one filler cover the range without new tooling.
- **Food.** High-SKU food plants change over several times a shift; tool-less quick change parts remove the wrench from that routine.
- **Household products.** Trigger sprays, ovals and bricks all need their own guides — colour-coded sets stop the wrong parts going on the machine.
- **Personal care.** Fine finishes and premium packaging are unforgiving: UHMW contact points and controlled finishing protect the container's appearance.
- **Automotive and petroleum.** F-style 0.95 L and 3.8 L bottles and aerosol cans push hard on guides. In-house manufacturing keeps replacements measured in days, not months.
