- **Alcohol.** Reject systems designed to integrate with both the OEM's and the end customer's control architecture, so the machine builder's platform stayed intact.
- **Beverage.** When another supplier's change parts failed to fit an OEM machine, on-site measurement through to international shipment took seven business days.
- **Dairy.** Screw systems built to feed cup and tub equipment at the pitch the downstream machine was designed to receive.
- **Food.** Metering systems feeding check weighing equipment at 10,800 containers per hour, built to the accuracy window the machine builder specified.
- **Household products.** Handling equipment specified upstream of filling and labelling so the OEM's machine receives containers already correctly oriented.
- **Personal care.** Feeding and orienting systems built for containers whose finish leaves no margin for mis-handling in the machine.
- **Automotive and petroleum.** Integrated inspection and mating systems developed jointly, where handling, inspection and assembly had to be solved as one machine.

## 9. Booth answers

**"We design our own change parts."**
Plenty of builders do. The question is whether it's the best use of your engineering hours, and who carries the cost when a part needs reworking.

**"What happens if the parts don't work?"**
They're ours. We take full ownership of the design, you pay a single price, and any necessary reworks are our responsibility.

**"Our machine constraints are tight."**
That's the normal brief. We manufacture to your prints, meeting the specifications your envelope requires, so handling and spacing work inside the machine as designed.

**"Ordering from you would slow us down."**
It shouldn't. Our engineers can build a custom catalogue of standard drive units matched to your equipment, so your team orders a part number.

**"We need parts fast for a customer."**
That's the partnership most OEMs value first — timing screws and change parts supplied quickly, manufactured in our own plant.

**"What's in it for our end user?"**
Better container control upstream of your machine, less wasted product, and fewer manual tasks around your system — which reflects on your equipment, not just ours.
