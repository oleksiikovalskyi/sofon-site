## 5. Extended paragraph (~700–900 chars)

V1) There are three drive unit platforms, and the choice between them comes down to how many points of control the container needs and how fast the line runs.

Single screw units handle rounds, ovals and rectangles with large radius corners: product spacing, discharge at a consistent pitch, dwell and conveyor transfer, and spacing for labelling, sleeving, vision inspection and check weighing. Dual synchro bar units add a second screw for containers needing more control, running continuous motion up to 18,000 containers per hour. Dual servo units take over above 18,000 per hour, for indexing applications and anywhere the unit has to synchronize with the rest of the line — with fewer mechanical parts as a result.

Every platform ships as a complete system, with guarding and controls packages available, and a backlog feed is recommended into all of them.  [859 chars]

## 7. Technical facts

**Three platforms**
- Single Screw Drive Unit — rounds, ovals, rectangles with large radius corners. Spacing, consistent discharge pitch, dwell, conveyor and product transfer, spacing for labelling, sleeving, vision inspection and check weighing.
- Dual Synchro Bar Drive Unit — rounds, ovals, rectangles. Continuous motion to 18,000 containers per hour. Consistent orientation on discharge, presenting ovals and rectangles to 0.4 mm of rotation in the direction of flow. Turning, rotating and grouping.
- Dual Servo Drive Unit — rounds, ovals, rectangles. For applications above 18,000 containers per hour, indexing, or synchronizing with other areas of the line. Reduced mechanical parts.

## 9. Booth answers

**"Which platform do we need?"**
Two questions decide it. How many points of control does the container need, and how fast are you running? Above 18,000 containers per hour, or indexing, or synchronizing with other equipment — that's servo. Below, and standing alone, synchro bar does it more simply.

**"Does it come with screws?"**
Yes. Every standard drive unit is supplied with the timing screws that perform your application. That's the point of the product.

**"We run four container sizes on that line."**
Screws change tool-lessly. If the centrelines have to move between sizes, add precision adjusters — in/out and up/down on numerical counters, so the setup repeats exactly next time.

**"Will it fight our line controls?"**
No. Controls packages are designed to run independent of existing line controls. Network and safety integration can be built in on request.

**"We're an OEM — can we standardize on this?"**
That's a normal arrangement. Our engineers can build a custom catalogue of standard drive units matched to your equipment, so ordering is a part number rather than a project.

**"It's a washdown environment."**
Washdown construction is available, and guarding can be supplied in all stainless steel rather than stainless and polycarbonate.
