# Trade show copy bank — brief and ground rules

**What this is.** Ready-to-place English copy for exhibition handouts and stand
graphics, drawn from an existing body of product and application content.
Text only — no image selection, no layout.

**How to use it.** Every direction is a *bank of blocks*, not a finished text.
Each block gives several interchangeable variants. The designer picks one variant
per block and assembles them to fit the layout. Blocks are ordered from largest
type size to smallest.

## Block structure (same in every file)

| Block | What it is | Target length |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Headlines | Main line of the panel or handout | 20–60 chars |
| 2. Subheads | Second line under the headline | 50–110 chars |
| 3. Short paragraph | Caption under an image, sidebar | ~150 chars |
| 4. Standard paragraph | Main body of a section | ~300–350 chars |
| 5. Extended paragraph | Full page or back-of-leaflet copy | ~700–900 chars |
| 6. Benefit bullets | Bulleted list, parallel phrasing | 25–60 chars each |
| 7. Technical facts | Numbers, materials, models, coverage | varies |
| 8. Industry proof points | One line per industry | 90–160 chars each |
| 9. Booth answers | Replies to the questions visitors actually ask | 1–3 sentences |
| 10. CTAs | Buttons, QR captions, closing lines | 15–45 chars |
| 11. Micro-copy | Badges, stickers, labels, icon captions | 8–30 chars |

Variants are numbered `V1)`, `V2)` … with the exact character count in brackets
at the end of the line, so text can be fitted to a layout without recounting.

## Ground rules applied throughout

- **English only.**
- **No customer names and no product brands.** Named companies are never used.
  Examples appear anonymized by industry —
  "a household cleaning products manufacturer", "a winery", never the brand.
- **Facts come from the source material only.** No invented speeds, materials
  or claims. Where the source material gives a number, it is used verbatim.
- **No commercial terms.** Discount percentages tied to maintenance agreements
  are deliberately left out of print materials. Available on request.
- **Throughput is stated in containers per hour.** The source material states speeds
  in containers per minute (CPM); every figure here is that number × 60. Where a
  line quotes several speeds, the first is written out in full and the rest are
  shortened — "14,400 containers per hour per lane, 28,800 per hour total".
  Micro-copy badges use the short form, "48,000/hour".
- **All units are metric.** US fluid ounces converted to ml and L, weight ounces
  to grams, US quart to 0.95 L, US gallon to 3.8 L, pint to 0.5 L, fractional
  inches to mm. Container volumes are nominal pack sizes, rounded sensibly for
  print — 240 ml, 415 ml, 950 ml rather than 236.6, 414.0, 946.4.
- Wording is new marketing copy written from the source content — not a verbatim
  quote of it, so the same page can be reused online without duplicate text.

## Scope

**In scope, first priority:** Change Parts · Timing Screws / Feed Screws ·
Down Bottle Reject Systems.

**In scope, second priority:** Screw Drive Units · Custom / Engineered-to-Order ·
Engineering and Integration · Contract Manufacturing · Aftermarket Service ·
OEM Partnership.

**Industries in scope (7):** Alcohol · Beverage · Cosmetics and Personal Care ·
Dairy · Food · Household Products · Automotive and Petroleum.

**Deliberately out of scope:** container handling machines, laning (combiners and
dividers), inverters and twist blocks, denesters, rinsers, can openers,
adjustable guide rail systems as a product line, strip label applicator, and the eight
remaining industries.
