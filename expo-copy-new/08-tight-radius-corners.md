## 8. Industry proof points

- **Beverage.** High-speed corners are where lightweight containers tip first; millimetre-accurate rails on both sides keep them upright through the bend.
- **Food.** Multi-SKU lines change corner settings constantly — a displayed number replaces the trial-and-error pass that usually follows.
- **Pharmaceutical.** Small containers on tight-radius corners need precise dual-sided control, in a washdown-capable, maintenance-free assembly.
- **Personal care.** Tall, narrow and decorated containers travel corners badly; low-friction, non-marking rail contact protects the finish.
- **Household chemicals.** Full containers behave differently from empty ones around a bend, and the same corner handles both without re-engineering.
- **Automotive and lubricants.** Heavy containers put real load into corner rails; robust mounts and couplings hold the setting under it.
- **Contract packaging.** Corners stop being a change part inventory problem — one adjustable corner covers the container range instead of a rail set per SKU.
