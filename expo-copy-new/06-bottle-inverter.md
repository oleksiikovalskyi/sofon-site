- **Beverage.** Cap sterilisation and base coding both need inversion; one modular unit covers both rather than two devices competing for conveyor space.
- **Food.** Debris shakeout before filling protects product integrity, and modular construction means the unit comes apart for cleaning between runs.
- **Pharmaceutical.** Controlled, repeatable inversion with easy washdown suits environments where cleaning between products is non-negotiable.
- **Personal care.** Premium containers scuff easily; a variable speed belt drive lets the handling be tuned to the finish rather than the throughput target.
- **Household chemicals.** Container lay-down and inversion for coding are routine, and washdown-friendly modular construction handles the chemistry.
- **Automotive and lubricants.** Heavier containers benefit from back-pressure-driven inversion that doesn't rely on gripping the container.
- **Contract packaging.** No measure-up and no custom parts means the same inverter serves a new customer's container without a redesign.
