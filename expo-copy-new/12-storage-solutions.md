V2) There are two sensible ways to solve change part storage, and they suit different plants.

Custom 5S carts are engineered around your parts: shadow boards cut to the part outline, custom engraving, purpose-built drawers, doors and covers, and mounts specified for what they hold. They suit regulated and lean environments, and cleanroom-compliant construction is available.

Configurable carts take the opposite approach: standard robust mounts and bins that snap securely into mesh, in three standard lengths of 2,390 mm, 1,780 mm and 1,170 mm, which you configure and reconfigure yourself. They store more parts per square metre than competitive systems, need no measure-up, and can be repurposed when the line changes.

Both are washdown ready, both offer wall-mounted variations, and both remove the hunt from the changeover.  [829 chars]

- **Beverage.** High changeover frequency multiplies every minute spent looking for a part; shadow boards make the correct set self-evident.
- **Food.** Washdown-ready carts let parts be cleaned where they are stored, which is usually what makes cleaning actually happen.
- **Pharmaceutical.** Cleanroom-compliant carts, enclosed cabinets and removable shadow board inserts suit regulated storage and cleaning requirements.
- **Personal care.** Large portfolios mean large part inventories — vertical storage and configurable mounts keep the footprint down.
- **Household chemicals.** One manufacturer recovered 30 minutes per changeover and 120 minutes of weekly downtime through storage discipline alone.
- **Automotive and lubricants.** Heavy metal parts are moved to the machine on the cart rather than carried, which takes real ergonomic risk off the floor.
- **Contract packaging.** Parts for many customers on one site need unambiguous separation; engraving, shadow boards and colour-coding provide it.
