- **Beverage.** Case packer infeeds change with every pack format; adjusting seven lanes from one point turns a format change into a setting change.
- **Food.** High-SKU plants run many pack configurations on the same case packer — lane count and width both adjust, so one infeed covers them all.
- **Pharmaceutical.** Removing change parts and templates removes a documented-storage and cleaning burden from a regulated area.
- **Personal care.** Wide container ranges across a brand portfolio need frequent lane resets; mid-run variance adjustments take seconds.
- **Household chemicals.** Large and small formats share the same accumulation lanes without a spacer set for each.
- **Automotive and lubricants.** Heavy containers in multi-lane accumulation are handled without lifting equipment on the changeover.
- **Contract packaging.** Every new customer brings a new pack format; adjustable lanes mean no new change parts to design, buy and store.
