## 8. Industry proof points

- **Beverage.** High-speed infeeds live or die on singulation. Screw geometry proven in simulation keeps containers separated at line speed without contact damage.
- **Food.** Hot-fill and multi-SKU work needs the same screw to behave the same way every run — pocket-to-pocket consistency is what makes that repeatable.
- **Pharmaceutical.** Small vials and containers demand precise metering into the machine, with surfaces and materials suited to a regulated environment.
- **Personal care.** Complex geometry — angled necks, oval and asymmetric bodies — is exactly where a custom screw earns its place over a standard one.
- **Household chemicals.** Anti-static and chemically inert material options handle aggressive products without adding friction or static build-up.
- **Automotive and lubricants.** Heavy, large-format containers separate reliably when the screw is designed for their mass rather than their diameter.
- **Pet care.** Large, awkward containers such as litter packs need precise spacing and orientation before filling; that is a screw-design problem.

## 9. Booth answers

**"Our containers shingle at the infeed."**
That's the classic feed screw job. Separation is built into the screw geometry and proven in simulation before we make it, so it works on your container rather than on an average one.

**"Our container isn't round."**
Rectangular, oval, angled-neck and asymmetric containers are routine. The screw is designed around the container, not the other way around.

**"How long does a changeover take?"**
With quick-change mounts, it's tool-less — the screw comes off and the next one goes on. Colour-coding means the operator picks the right one first time.

**"Will it mark our labels?"**
Surfaces are smooth and low-friction specifically to avoid scuffing and marking. That's a material and finish decision, and we make it deliberately.

**"Can you match the drive we already have?"**
Yes. Screws are designed for any machine make and model, and we also build the drive units if you want the pair engineered together.

**"How do you know it will time correctly?"**
Because we simulate container travel and test before manufacture. Pocket-to-pocket accuracy is proven, not hoped for.
