## 8. Industry proof points

- **Beverage.** Lightweight containers need rail settings held to a millimetre; single-location adjustment makes that repeatable across the whole line, not just the easy sections.
- **Food.** Frequent SKU changes turn rail adjustment into a daily cost. Adjusting 24 metres from one point converts it into a ten-second task.
- **Pharmaceutical.** Quarantined and hard-to-access areas can be adjusted from a single accessible location, without entry or lifting equipment.
- **Personal care.** Complex bottle geometry and premium finishes benefit from beaded rail profiles that reduce friction and static in transit.
- **Household chemicals.** Anti-static material options and low-friction profiles handle products where scuffing or static build-up is a real risk.
- **Automotive and lubricants.** Heavy containers and wide size ranges are handled by a rail system rigid enough for the load and infinitely adjustable across the range.
- **Contract packaging.** Infinite adjustment means the next customer's container is already covered — no new change parts, no new templates.
